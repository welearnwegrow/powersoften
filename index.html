About the Game

Powers of Ten: Our Universe is an educational card-sorting game that takes players on a journey from the smallest known lengths in physics to the scale of the observable universe.

This digital version is based on the original physical card game created by Jaya Ramchandani, published on AstroEDU under a Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International License.

The game helps players learn about powers of ten, orders of magnitude, and the incredible scale of our cosmos — from the Planck length (10⁻³⁵ m) to the co-moving diameter of the observable universe (10²⁶ m).
